# [Greatest of Three Numbers](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/problems/greatest-of-three-numbers2520/1)
## Easy
Given three numbers a, b and c. Find the greatest number among them.Examples:Input: a = 10, b = 3, c = 2
Output: 10
Explanation: 10 is greatest among the three Input: a = -4, b = -3, c = -2
Output: -2
Explanation: -2 is greatest among the three
